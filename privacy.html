TALOS Privacy Policy

TALOS is a private assistant used only by its owner. It accesses Google account data (such as Gmail, Calendar and Drive) solely on behalf of that single owner.

No data is collected from, shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. Data is stored only on the owner's own device. TALOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
